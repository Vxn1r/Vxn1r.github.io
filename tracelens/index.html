05 / METHOD & LIMITS

How TraceLens works

TRANSPARENT BY DESIGN
01

Passive DNS only

TraceLens queries a fixed set of public DNS record types: A, AAAA, CNAME, MX, NS, TXT, CAA, and SOA. It does not enumerate subdomains, scan ports, probe services, or attempt authentication.

02

Resolver transparency

Lookups use Cloudflare DNS-over-HTTPS, with Google Public DNS as a fallback. The chosen resolver receives the domain query. Do not submit private or sensitive names.

03

Evidence stays local

Snapshots and notes are stored in this browser's local storage. There is no TraceLens account, remote database, analytics beacon, or server-side collection. Export your vault to keep a backup.

04

Observed is not proven

DNS records are time-stamped observations. They do not prove ownership, affiliation, vulnerability, physical location, or control of an address. A missing record does not prove non-existence.

Responsible use

Use only with domains you own or are authorized to assess. TraceLens is designed for learning, public-data verification, and organizing domain-level evidence—not identifying or tracking private individuals.
